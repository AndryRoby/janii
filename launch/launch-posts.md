## Facts for the owner (use these, don't improvise numbers)

- **What Janii is, in one line:** a free, offline Windows beta that reads a folder of RAW/JPEG wedding photographs, groups them into moments, and proposes a story (best-of, covers the day) and memories (story plus enough more that nobody present is missing), with a stated reason per frame.
- **What it is not, yet:** no straighten/crop/grade, Windows only, CPU only (a big wedding can take hours), no face recognition (legal, not technical, blocker), beta.
- **Price:** free during the beta. After the beta, one-time licence 169 EUR; first ten photographers who send feedback during the beta get first offer on it.
- **Download:** https://github.com/AndryRoby/janii/releases/latest (~130 MB ZIP).
- **Landing page:** https://arling.sk/janii/ · **Docs:** https://arling.sk/janii/docs/
- **Contact for feedback:** andrej@arling.sk

## Live threads and communities (real, checked 2026-09-06)

### 1. Adobe Community: "AI Culling" Lightroom Classic feature-request thread
https://community.adobe.com/t5/lightroom-classic-ideas/p-ai-culling/idi-p/13738692

A long-running, multi-page official Adobe feature-request thread where photographers (event and wedding shooters explicitly among them) ask Adobe to build automatic culling into Lightroom. Real quotes pulled from the thread:

> "2,000 to 3000 captures down to appx 400-600 deliverables"

> "Subject in focus or large part of the image in focus, Exposure within the histogram" (a commenter's wish-list for what an automatic culler should check)

Wildlife and sports shooters in the same thread stress the result has to work **offline, no cloud upload** (the same claim Janii can back up concretely: Janii has no network code at all, not just a privacy policy saying so). One commenter pushed back that Adobe should fix bugs before adding features, so read the room before posting; this is Adobe's own turf, not a place to sell a competing tool outright. Best use: reply with something genuinely useful (e.g. which RAW formats read cleanly, or how the "no upload" claim is enforced) only if the thread is still active when you check it, and expect it to read as "someone building the thing you're all asking for wrote a free beta," not an ad.

### 2. Fstoppers: "The AI Debate Splitting Wedding Photographers Right Now"
https://fstoppers.com/wedding/ai-debate-splitting-wedding-photographers-right-now-903674
By Alex Cooke, published 22 July 2026. Fstoppers has an active comments section and a separate community/groups area (https://fstoppers.com/groups) for photographers to share work and start discussions.

The article itself draws a line wedding photographers care about: **assistive AI** (culling, retouching) versus **generative AI** (fabricating content), and argues assistive tools are a legitimate labour-saver, not a craft shortcut. Real quotes:

> Photographer John Branch IV "still culls his own weddings because AI can't know the quirky, meaningful frames from the day, though he'll lean on AI culling for smaller shoots like engagement sessions."

> Commenter Randy Whiddon, on why some automated cleanup is necessary regardless: "if you capture that perfect moment but have a clear-as-day water bottle or a bright orange purse hanging on a chair - those have to get removed for high end albums."

Janii's honesty section ("what it does not do yet": no crop/grade, selection only) fits this exact debate well: it's explicitly *assistive*, not generative, and says so. A comment on this article, or a new post in Fstoppers' photography groups linking to the article and to Janii's own "what it does not do" section, is a natural, non-salesy fit.

### 4. r/WeddingPhotography (Reddit)
https://www.reddit.com/r/WeddingPhotography/

Large, active subreddit for working wedding photographers. **Its current self-promotion rules could not be verified live** (Reddit blocks the automated fetch tools available here), so before posting: open the subreddit, read the sidebar/rules and the pinned posts yourself, and check whether self-promotion is confined to a specific weekly thread (common on photography subreddits of this size) or requires mod permission first. ### 5. GitHub: open-source RAW/culling tools worth watching
- **RapidRAW** (https://github.com/CyberTimon/RapidRAW): a free, open-source, GPU-accelerated RAW editor that shipped a new "Culling View" (side-by-side comparison, star ratings) in July 2026. No open issue there currently asks for wedding-specific moment-grouping or a "story vs memories" split (checked 2026-09-06), so this is a "watch and maybe contribute to the conversation later" lead, not a live thread to jump into today.
- **Imagen AI Culling** (https://github.com/Imagen-AI-Culling/): a commercial competitor's public org on GitHub. Worth knowing about as positioning context, not a place to post.
